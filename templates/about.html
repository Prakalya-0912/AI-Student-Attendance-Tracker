1. Project Overview & Problem Statement

Traditional college attendance monitoring systems only record roll calls statically, often leaving faculty and mentors unaware of chronic absenteeism, consecutive missed lectures, or deteriorating attendance trends until semester-end detention lists are compiled.

The AI Student Attendance Tracker is an intelligent decision-support web application designed to bridge this gap. By utilizing a trained Machine Learning model alongside real-time attendance analytics, the system dynamically calculates attendance percentages, detects consecutive absence patterns, predicts student detention risk, and suggests tailored academic intervention timelines before students fall into critical categories.

3. Explainable Risk Scoring Formula

The prototype risk metric produces an intuitive 0 to 100 Risk Score calculated using weighted domain indicators:

Risk = (100 - Attendance) × 0.40
     + min(20, Consecutive_Absences × 5)
     + (100 - 10Day_Trend) × 0.15
     + (100 - Marks) × 0.08
     + (100 - Assignments) × 0.07
     + (100 - Activity) × 0.10
  • 0 – 29 (SAFE): Regular academic progress.
  • 30 – 59 (AT RISK): Requires mentor review.
  • 60 – 100 (CRITICAL): Immediate detention alert.
